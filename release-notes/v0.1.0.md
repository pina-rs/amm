### What's new

#### Trade and provide liquidity on the Pina AMM

_Owner:_ [@ifiokjr](https://github.com/ifiokjr) · _Review:_ [PR #1](https://github.com/pina-rs/amm/pull/1)

##### User impact

You can create a market for any two Solana tokens, trade between them for a small fee, and earn a share of those fees by adding liquidity. Fees are fixed when a market is created and can never be raised afterwards. Token creators can earn an ongoing fee from trading in their market, paid in the token of their choice.
